Profile

Industrial designer with a Bachelor of Industrial Design (Honours) from RMIT, majoring in Advanced Manufacturing, two years’ industrial design experience across freelance client work and a studio technician role at RMIT, and seven years’ experience as a swim coach, surf lifesaver and volunteer. I chose industrial design because I wanted to work in a field where I could be creative, practical and help people through design. I work end to end, from user research and CAD through electronics, interface and working prototypes, and I am especially interested in medical technology and designing products that can make people’s lives easier. I have strong experience working with people, supporting children of all abilities, staying calm under pressure and being part of a community-focused team.
