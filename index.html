Concussion Detection Mouthguard

An IMU reads abnormal force; a red and blue LED flashes on the field while Bluetooth alerts the bench in real time, so subtle, dangerous hits are no longer missed.

Year: 2026✦Role: Sole designer✦Tools: CAD, IMU/BLE✦Type: Honours project
About this project

A Design Honours project tackling concussions missed on the AFL field, where fewer than 5% of players wear helmets and sideline checks often miss subtle or delayed symptoms. I researched existing sensor mouthguards and biosensors, spoke with AFL medical staff, and tested materials, sensors and layouts to land on a mouthguard that combines impact sensors, LEDs and Bluetooth for instant visual alerts to players and off-ground staff, backed by a real-time dashboard for medical decision-making.

Highlights

Grounded in AFL concussion data & medical-staff interviews

Impact sensor + LED + Bluetooth alert system

Real-time dashboard for off-ground medical response

Fits existing AFL gameplay without changing how players move

Roof Top Green

A systems-mapping project transforming a high-emission student accommodation rooftop into a metabolic, participatory ecological garden.

Discipline: Systems & sustainable design✦Focus: Rooftop garden✦Lens: Metabolism · participation✦Type: RMIT studio brief
About this project

Roof Top Green reframes how CO₂ emissions form inside D2, a transient student accommodation building with low resident agency and no green infrastructure. Systems mapping across actor relationships, metabolic flows, pain points, an iceberg model and causal loops surfaced two leverage points: resident participation and building metabolism. The response is a rooftop garden of native plants, a pond, insect and bird habitat, and a shaded pergola that sequesters carbon while turning residents into active caretakers of the building’s ecology.

Highlights

Full systems mapping: actors, metabolism, pain points, iceberg & causal loops

Native plants, pond and habitat structures for CO₂ sequestration

Designed to build resident participation, not just infrastructure

Neo Shoreline

A business plan for premium beach and leisure products made from up-cycled neoprene wetsuit offcuts, starting with the NeoBean Bag, a water-resistant beanbag built almost entirely from waste material.

Role: Marketing & material sourcing✦Team: 4-person venture✦Year: 2024
About this project

Neoprene can’t be recycled and takes centuries to break down, yet manufacturing wetsuits and other neoprene goods leaves large amounts of offcut waste. Neo Shoreline is a business plan built around sourcing that waste from surf/dive shops, wetsuit manufacturers and social media call-outs, and turning it into the NeoBean Bag, a beanbag whose outer panels use higher-grade offcuts and whose filling is shredded from lower-grade scrap. The plan covers user personas, market analysis, sourcing, manufacturing process and financials.

Highlights

Full-use design: high-grade offcuts for panels, low-grade shredded for filling

3-channel neoprene sourcing: shops, manufacturers, social call-outs

User personas & market analysis for beach/outdoor buyers

Costed financial plan targeting $100k in seed funding
